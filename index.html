Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a service, select quantity, and press “Order via Support”. Your order details will be prepared for you to send through Telegram or WhatsApp.

Is the stock count live?

The displayed quantities are sample values stored in the website code. GitHub Pages is static, so stock does not automatically update across customers. Update the product list in script.js or connect a secure backend for live inventory.

Are payments automatic?

No. This starter website provides payment method information and manual support links. Automatic payment verification requires a merchant integration and secure backend.

Do you need my password or OTP?

No. Never share your password, OTP, recovery code, or private account credentials with anyone.
